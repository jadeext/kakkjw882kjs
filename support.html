Quick Fixes

Why doesn't the menu show?

Install the missing runtimes:
DirectX End-User Runtime
.NET 10.0 Runtime

Why is the ESP laggy?

Open the menu → disable V-Sync → raise “Overlay FPS” to your liking.

Loader closes right after login

Delete C:\Jade\credentials.dat and log in again.
